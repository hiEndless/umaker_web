# UMAKER Website Kit

## Homepage Direction

The homepage should be spacious, technical, and readable. It should not look like a terminal dumped onto a landing page.

Recommended first-screen structure:

```text
UMAKER
The Intelligence Layer for Quantitative Trading
Structure the market. Discover factors. Drive systematic strategies.

[Explore System] [Request Access]

Semantic particle topology:
Market Data -> Market Structure -> Factor -> Signal
```

## Scroll Narrative

Use scroll animation to reduce pressure:

- Reveal one concept at a time.
- Keep particle systems sparse.
- Use line drawing, node activation, opacity, and small translations.
- Do not keep all metrics visible at once on the homepage.

## Page-Specific Density

Homepage: low.

Engine page: medium.

Strategy detail: medium, research report style.

Monitor: high, table and metrics first.
