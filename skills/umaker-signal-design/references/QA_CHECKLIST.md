# UMAKER Design QA Checklist

## Brand

- Does the page feel like quantitative intelligence infrastructure?
- Does it avoid generic AI SaaS, crypto bot, and cyberpunk aesthetics?
- Is cold blue/cyan carrying the technology atmosphere?
- Is emerald used as scarce UMAKER signal, not a dominant color field?
- Is the primary impression ice white, deep graphite, cold blue, and precise structure?

## Data

- Are live, paper, backtest, simulated, demo, and research states clearly distinguished?
- Are financial positive/negative colors separate from brand signal colors?
- Are fake returns, Sharpe, drawdown, or live claims avoided?

## Layout

- Is the homepage low-density enough?
- Are cards used only where they carry real structure?
- Does scrolling reveal concepts step by step?
- Do particle visuals represent relationships?

## Motion

- Does every animation explain system state or narrative progression?
- Is reduced motion supported where implemented?
- Are glow and pulse effects restrained?
