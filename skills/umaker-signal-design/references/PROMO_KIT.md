# UMAKER Promo Kit

## Direction

Promotional assets should look like a premium scientific instrument brand, not a trading-profit advertisement.

Use:

- Ice white wordmark
- Graphite black canvas
- Cold blue technology atmosphere
- Cyan particle or data-flow lines
- Silver-gray construction lines
- Sparse emerald signal nodes
- Precise square boundaries
- Minimal copy

Avoid:

- ROI claims
- Candles as the main visual
- Coins
- Robots
- AI brains
- Purple gradients
- Bright green backgrounds
- Green-dominant poster systems

## Poster Layouts

Recommended copy:

```text
UMAKER
MARKET INTELLIGENCE CORE
```

or:

```text
UMAKER
STRUCTURE · FACTOR · SIGNAL
```

Keep emerald as 3 to 8 small accent points, not as the main visual mass. Blue and cyan may carry the primary spatial and technology feeling.
